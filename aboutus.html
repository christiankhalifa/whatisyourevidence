About Us

Honest questions, handled with care.

This is the heart behind everything here. We think faith and evidence belong together, and that the biggest questions deserve careful, kind, and honest answers — the kind you can look into for yourself.

Our starting point is love — for God, and for the people we talk with:

Matthew 22:39 · Jesus

“Love your neighbour as yourself.”

So we aim to be gentle and genuinely respectful in every conversation:

1 Peter 3:15 · NIV

“Always be prepared to give an answer… But do this with gentleness and respect.”

And we care about evidence, because the Christian faith rests on real events in history:

1 Corinthians 15:14

“If Christ has not been raised, our preaching is useless and so is your faith.”

Honest about evidence. Gentle with people.

We go to primary sources, history, and Scripture, and we lay out what we find as clearly as we can — so you can weigh it for yourself. If you think we've got something wrong, we'd love to hear it; good questions make everyone's thinking sharper. The goal was never to win an argument. It's to help clear a path toward Jesus, together.

Statement of Faith

Bible-believing, born-again, Trinitarian.

I am a BBT — Bible-believing, born-again, Trinitarian Christian. I affirm the full authority of Scripture, salvation by grace through faith alone, the deity of Christ, His bodily resurrection, and the historic Christian faith.
